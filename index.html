🕒 Temporal Architecture

Why Practice with a Visual Analog Exam Timer?

Standard countdown clocks force candidates to continually perform mental arithmetic (e.g. "I have 47 minutes left for 31 questions—how many seconds per question is that?").Exam Timer Online maps elapsed and remaining duration onto high-contrast angular sweep sectors. This engages peripheral vision and eliminates time blindness during high-stakes competitive examinations.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Exam Timer Online

How does Exam Timer Online help simulate real test conditions?

Exam Timer Online replicates physical examination rooms with analog sweep clocks, strict mode (pause-lock), customizable milestone chimes (e.g. at 15m, 10m, and 5m remaining), and a clean distraction-free fullscreen mode that mirrors official test hall conditions.

Does the countdown timer keep running if I switch tabs or lose internet?

Yes. The state engine calculates remaining time using true timestamp differentials (Date.now()) instead of fragile interval counters. It runs seamlessly offline via Service Worker Progressive Web App (PWA) caching.

Can I customize alarm sounds and milestone warnings?

Yes. You can choose from 6 synthesized tones (Classic Ding-Dong, Digital Beep, School Bell, Siren, Gentle Gong, Fanfare), add custom warning minute marks, and adjust master sound volume in the Settings drawer.

What keyboard shortcuts are available?

You can control the clock completely with your keyboard: Space (Pause / Resume), F (Fullscreen), M (Mute / Unmute), E (End Exam), ] or → (Next Question), [ or ← (Previous Question), and Esc (Close modals/drawers).

Is my exam history saved locally?

Yes, all session metrics and summaries are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. No private data is ever sent to any remote server.
