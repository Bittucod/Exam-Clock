🧠 Cognitive Time Science

Why Visual Analog Sweep Beats Digital Numbers

Reading numeric digital digits (e.g. 01:37:42) requires active focal attention and mental subtraction to calculate remaining proportion. This micro-distraction consumes precious prefrontal cortex working memory.

By contrast, an analog clock face engages peripheral vision. A candidate glancing sideways immediately perceives angular wedge slices (e.g. "half the dial is remaining") without interrupting mathematical calculation or textual reading comprehension.

❌ Raw Phone / Digital Stopwatch

  • Forces mental arithmetic to gauge fraction of time left
  • Constant screen notifications and interruptions
  • Encourages clock-watching and test anxiety
  • Unrealistic compared to real physical exam halls

✅ Exam Timer Online Clock

  • Visual colored sectors show exact remaining proportion
  • Distraction-free fullscreen mode with zero notifications
  • Customizable audio milestone chimes (15m, 10m, 5m warnings)
  • Works 100% offline via Progressive Web App (PWA)

Common Questions About Getting Started

Does the exam timer keep ticking if I switch tabs or minimize the browser?

Yes, 100%. Exam Timer Online does not rely on fragile browser interval ticks that throttle when minimized. It tracks absolute UTC system timestamps. When you switch back or refresh, the clock immediately renders the exact millisecond-accurate remaining duration.

Can I use this timer without an internet connection?

Yes! This web application is a verified Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded in your browser, all audio files, SVG clock faces, and state management scripts are cached on your device. You can run full mocks on an airplane or in isolated study rooms with zero internet connectivity.

What audio warning chimes are included?

You can enable audible warnings at key time milestones (e.g. 15 minutes, 10 minutes, and 5 minutes remaining). Choose between Classic Ding-Dong, Digital Electronic Beeps, Traditional School Bell, Urgent Siren, Deep Gong, and Fanfare.
