Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosure

Your academic study and mock test attempts belong exclusively to you. Exam Timer Online does not track, store, or transmit your personal data.

1. Summary (TL;DR)

Zero Tracking, Zero Accounts, Zero Cloud Logging: Exam Timer Online does not require user registration, does not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, and never uploads your exam scores or elapsed times to any remote server.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike commercial education platforms, we deliberately refuse to collect:

  • Personal identification details (no names, telephone numbers, or physical addresses).
  • Authentication credentials (no passwords or social logins).
  • Individual question performance or academic grades.
  • Cross-site advertising profiles or behavioral browsing history.

3. Cookie Policy & Local Storage Transparency

Exam Timer Online does not use tracking cookies or third-party marketing cookies.

To provide seamless persistent functionality, our application utilizes standard browser LocalStorage technology. The following keys are stored exclusively inside your own browser:

LocalStorage KeyFunctional PurposeStorage Location
exam-clock-settingsStores your preferred alarm chime, volume levels, clock theme, and warning alert minutes.Local Browser Only
exam-clock-presetsSaves custom examination duration presets you create using the "Save Current" button.Local Browser Only
exam-clock-historyStores your personal log of completed/stopped mock test attempts (date, duration, completion %).Local Browser Only
exam-clock-themeRemembers your Dark Mode or Light Mode preference between sessions.Local Browser Only

Note: You can delete all locally saved data at any moment by clicking the "Clear History" button in the History drawer, or by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Caching

Exam Timer Online uses a native browser Service Worker to cache static assets (HTML, SVG dial graphics, CSS, and audio synthesis scripts). This allows the application to launch and run seamlessly without an active internet connection. No tracking or telemetry is performed via the Service Worker.

5. Children's Privacy

Because our platform collects no personal identifiable information whatsoever, Exam Timer Online is fully compliant with global student privacy regulations, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6. Policy Updates & Contact

If we make minor technical refinements to our offline caching or local storage mechanisms, updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

For questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact our team at privacy@examtimer.online or through our Contact Page.
